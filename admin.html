🔄 MEN’S QUADRUPLE — 60 POINTS

  • The match continues until one team reaches 60 points. A score of 60–59 is valid.
  • Pair changes are triggered by the first team to reach 15, 30 or 45 points. When one team reaches the threshold, both teams change pairs.
  • Rotation: 1 + 2 → 2 + 3 → 3 + 4 → 4 + 1.
  • Score ranges: 0–14, 15–29, 30–44, 45–59.
  • There is no 2-point advantage rule for the Quadruple.

🏆 FINALS TEAMS

The PCBL Finals are played between only 2 teams: the top 2 teams from the League-stage standings.

✓ The Finals fixtures can only be created for those two League finalists.

👥 PLAYER UNIQUENESS — FINALS MATCHES 2, 3 & 4

Across Finals Matches 2, 3 and 4, all 9 men must be unique. Example: Match 2 = A2, A3, A8, A9; Match 3 = A1; Match 4 = A4, A5, A6, A7.

✓ This uses A1–A9 exactly once across Matches 2, 3 and 4.

🏆 OVERALL SCORING

Points scored by each team in every Finals match are added to the cumulative Finals score. After all 6 matches, the team with the highest score out of 225 is the PCBL Finals Champion.

PCBL RULES

PCBL Rules

Pairing, triplet, backup and badminton edge-case rules for PCBL.

🤝 PAIRING RULES

  • Doubles pairing must be unique in every round.
  • The same two players cannot form a doubles pair again in a subsequent round.
  • Repeated pairing penalty: If the same two players are paired again, whether by mistake or deliberately, the opposite team is awarded 1 point.
  • Every player should play only once in each round, unless an approved unavailability rule requires a repeat.
  • Pairings should be planned across all rounds to maximize variety and fairness.

📝 PAIRING SUBMISSION & PLAYING ORDER

  • Before every match, each team must submit its complete player pairing and playing sequence to the umpire.
  • The submission must clearly mention Women’s Doubles pairing, Triplet players and 15+15 sequence, all Men’s Doubles pairings, and the order in which the pairs will play.
  • Once submitted, the pairing and playing sequence is final for that match.

👨‍👨‍👦 TRIPLET RULES — 30 POINTS

  • A Triplet is a 30-point match with a deuce rule, split after the first 15 total rally points. From 30 points onward, a 2-point lead is required; 40 points wins.
  • The 1st nominated player is OUT after 15 total rally points, the 2nd nominated player is COMMON and plays all 30 points, and the 3rd nominated player is IN for the second phase.
  • Example: Team A selects A9 → A1 → A2: Phase 1 = A9 + A1; Phase 2 = A1 + A2. Team B follows the same order.
  • The same two players cannot be the active pair again in a subsequent round; repeated pairing, whether by mistake or deliberately, gives 1 point to the opposite team.

🚫 PLAYER UNAVAILABILITY

1 PLAYER UNAVAILABLE
  • 8 players remain.
  • Form 4 unique pairs.
  • 3 pairs play Men’s Doubles.
  • The 4th combination is used for the 30-point Triplet.
  • Pairing rules still apply.
2 PLAYERS UNAVAILABLE
  • 7 players remain.
  • One suitable player may repeat to create the required combinations.
  • Maintain 4 unique pairs wherever possible.
  • The repeating player should not create a previously used pair.
  • The 4th combination is used for the 30-point Triplet.
3 PLAYERS UNAVAILABLE
  • 6 players remain.
  • The Triplet becomes a BYE for the opposition.

🛡️ BACKUP PLAYER RULE

  • If any player backs out before the tournament, the designated backup player will join the team if available.
  • Once the backup player joins, they become a regular team player for the tournament.
  • The same pairing and Triplet rules apply to the backup player.

🏸 BADMINTON DOUBLES — EDGE CASE RULES

Service
  • Server misses the shuttle while attempting to serve → Fault; receiver wins the rally.
  • Shuttle touches the net during service and lands in the correct service court → Legal service; play continues.
  • Shuttle touches the net during service but does not cross → Fault.
  • Shuttle lands on the service line → In.
  • Shuttle lands outside the service court → Out.
  • Wrong player receives → Fault on the receiving side.
  • Server serves from wrong service court → Fault.
  • Receiver moves before the serve → Fault.
  • Server lifts a foot before striking → Fault.
  • Serve above legal service height → Fault.
During the rally
  • Shuttle touches the net and goes over → Legal; play continues.
  • Shuttle rolls along the top of the net and falls on opponent’s side → Legal if it reaches the opponent’s side.
  • Shuttle gets stuck in the net → Rally ends; side responsible loses the rally.
  • Shuttle lands exactly on any boundary line → In.
  • Shuttle hits ceiling, wall, light, fan or another obstacle → Fault.
  • Shuttle touches player’s body/clothing → Fault.
  • Shuttle touches racket frame instead of strings → Legal.
  • Shuttle is hit twice by same player → Fault.
  • Shuttle accidentally touches both partners’ rackets → Fault.
  • Players hit shuttle simultaneously → Fault.
  • Shuttle is carried or slung on racket → Fault.
  • Player deliberately catches shuttle → Fault.
Net rules
  • Player touches net with racket, body or clothing → Fault.
  • Racket crosses over the net after hitting shuttle → Legal if the shuttle was first struck on the player’s own side.
  • Player reaches over the net before hitting shuttle → Fault.
  • Racket follows through over the net after legal contact → Legal.
  • Player’s body crosses under the net without obstructing opponent → Legal.
  • Player obstructs opponent under the net → Fault.
Doubles-specific
  • Wrong player serves → Fault.
  • Wrong player receives → Fault.
  • Partner returns the serve instead of the designated receiver → Fault.
  • Partners collide → Play continues unless a fault occurs.
  • Partners’ rackets collide → Play continues if only one legal hit is made.
LET — REPLAY THE RALLYA let may be called for an agreed qualifying interruption such as the receiver not being ready and not attempting the return, both sides committing a fault simultaneously, shuttle break during play, external interference, or when the umpire stops play.

🏆 MATCH-WIN POINT SYSTEM

  • Every individual match win awards 1 team point.
  • Each team plays 5 team matches in the league stage, with 5 individual matches in each team match.
  • Therefore, 25 match points are available to each team across the league stage.
  • League standings are determined by total match-win points.
  • If teams finish level on points, the applicable PCBL tie-break procedure will be used.

📖 PCBL SCORING & COURT RULES

  • Each match is 1 set of 21 points — there is no best-of-3-games format in PCBL.
  • Rally-point scoring applies: the side winning each rally gets 1 point.
  • At 20-all, a side must gain a 2-point lead to win.
  • At 29-all, the side scoring the 30th point wins.
  • Court ends are changed when a side first reaches 11 points.
  • Doubles service and service-court rotation follow the Laws of Badminton, except where a specific PCBL rule overrides them.

PCBL-specific rules take precedence over standard BWF rules wherever they differ.
