PCBL Rules

Unlimited roster

Each team may have any number of registered players.

Match types

Men's Singles, Women's Singles, Men's Doubles, Women's Doubles and Mixed Doubles.

Lineups

Singles use one player per side; doubles use two. Match lineups are displayed once entered by the tournament scorer.

Scoring

Best of 3 games, 21-point badminton scoring. At 20-all, a 2-point lead is required, with a maximum of 30.

Standings

A completed match awards one match win to the winning team.
