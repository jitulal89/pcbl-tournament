Team event

Each fixture is a badminton match contributing one win to the team-vs-team tie.

Match types

Men's Singles, Women's Singles, Men's Doubles, Women's Doubles and Mixed Doubles.

Women’s Doubles scheduling

The schedule can place Women’s Doubles in the first 10-minute slot.

Player uniqueness

Matches 2, 3 and 4 can be validated against earlier lineups before a match starts.

Scoring

Best of 3 games, 21 points; at 20-all continue until a 2-point lead, capped at 30.
